Algorithmic Substitution Rules & Logic:
  1. Step 1 (Absence Detection): Identifies all instructional periods where the absent faculty member has assigned classes on the chosen day.
  2. Step 2 (Free Pool Extraction): Scans the master timetable to locate faculty members with free periods (class = 'null') during those exact slots.
  3. Step 3 (Clash-Free Assignment): Maps free faculty to absent periods, guaranteeing zero double-booking collisions, and compiles the color-coded substitution matrix.
